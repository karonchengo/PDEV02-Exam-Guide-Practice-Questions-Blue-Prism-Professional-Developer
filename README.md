## What Is the Blue Prism Professional Developer (PDEV02) Certification?

The Blue Prism Professional Developer certification is designed for developers who want to demonstrate advanced capabilities in building, maintaining, and supporting enterprise automation solutions. It represents a step beyond foundational development knowledge, emphasizing the ability to solve technical problems, validate automation behavior, support testing and deployment, and apply established development practices.

According to Blue Prism University, the certification evaluates candidates' ability to troubleshoot process execution, develop and document test procedures, and support operational teams through user acceptance testing (UAT) and deployment. These skills are particularly valuable for professionals working with complex business processes and production automation environments.

For developers seeking to expand their technical expertise, earning this certification can provide a structured way to validate their knowledge and demonstrate their commitment to professional development.

## Blue Prism Professional Developer (PDEV02) Exam Details

Before beginning your preparation, familiarize yourself with the current exam format and requirements.

- **Exam name:** Blue Prism Professional Developer
- **Exam code:** PDEV02
- **Number of questions:** 50
- **Exam duration:** 75 minutes
- **Exam language:** English
- **Passing score:** A scaled score of 600 or higher on a 200–800 scale
- **Prerequisite:** Passing the Blue Prism Developer exam
- **Recommended experience:** Completion of the Professional Developer Learning Plan and approximately one year of hands-on development experience are strongly recommended.

The passing score is a scaled score rather than a direct percentage of correct answers. Candidates should therefore focus on developing a solid understanding of the tested concepts instead of relying on a simple target number of correct responses.

## Blue Prism Professional Developer Exam Objectives

The current Professional Developer exam organizes its content into four major knowledge domains. Understanding their relative weight can help you prioritize your study time.

### 1. Object Development — 18%

This domain focuses on developing reusable business objects and applying sound object design principles. Candidates should understand how to create reliable interactions between Blue Prism and external applications.

Key areas to review include:
- Business object structure and design
- Reusable actions and application interactions
- Exception handling within business objects
- Application integration and automation reliability
- Object testing, debugging, and maintainability

Strong object development skills help reduce duplication, improve consistency, and make automation solutions easier to maintain.

### 2. Process Development — 42%

Process Development is the largest exam domain, accounting for 42% of the exam content. It deserves significant attention during preparation.

Candidates should be comfortable building, debugging, and improving processes that automate business activities. Preparation should cover topics such as:
- Process flow design and control
- Advanced exception handling
- Work queue management and transaction processing
- Data items, collections, and decision logic
- Process debugging and troubleshooting
- Reliable handling of unexpected application behavior
- Reusable components and development best practices

When reviewing this domain, focus on understanding why a particular design or control-flow decision is appropriate in a given scenario. Practical troubleshooting skills are more useful than memorizing isolated definitions.

### 3. Process Implementation — 22%

This domain addresses the transition from development to reliable implementation. Candidates should understand how to prepare automation solutions for deployment, testing, and ongoing operation.

Important preparation areas include:
- Solution design principles and implementation planning
- Deployment considerations
- Testing and validation procedures
- Credentials and secure configuration
- Integration through APIs and related services
- Operational readiness and support
- Identifying risks that could affect automation stability

Review how development decisions affect the behavior of an automation after it enters a production environment. Understanding dependencies, configuration, and deployment requirements can help you reason through implementation scenarios.

### 4. Leadership — 18%

The Leadership domain highlights the importance of collaboration, documentation, and effective support throughout the automation lifecycle.

Relevant areas include:
- Reviewing business requirements
- Understanding process documentation
- Supporting user acceptance testing
- Communicating technical issues
- Coordinating with operational teams
- Documenting solutions and test procedures
- Supporting deployment and operational handover

This domain demonstrates that professional development involves more than technical implementation. Developers must also help ensure that automation solutions meet business expectations and can be supported by the teams responsible for them.

The domain percentages above are based on the current Blue Prism University exam outline. Use them to guide your preparation, but make sure you review all four domains.
